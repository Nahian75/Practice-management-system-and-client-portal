# Security review: People and HR module

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Scope:** People / HR steps 16a to 16f, migrations `0059` to `0064` and `0073`,
`apps/api/src/hr/`, the staff, manager and HR screens in `apps/web/src/app/(staff)/hr/`,
`apps/web/src/app/(staff)/people/`, and `apps/web/src/app/(staff)/my-team/`, protected
HR object namespaces, category retention/disposal, and reviewed fixed exports.  
**Method:** Current primary legislation and official statutory guidance (UK GDPR /
Data Protection Act 2018, Working Time Regulations 1998 reg 16B, Home Office Right
to Work Checklist, Equality Act 2010 s.20, Acas Code of Practice on disciplinary
and grievance procedures, SRA Code of Conduct for Firms, and ICO Employment Practices
Guidance on records and worker health) were rechecked on 14 October 2026. Threat models
for insider access, former-manager privilege persistence, concurrent role revocation
races, leaver blockers, restricted health data isolation, confidential casework team
boundaries, legal holds, disposal tombstones, and fixed reviewed exports were audited.
Confirmed defects received database hardening in migration `0073` and test regressions
before final verification.

## Regulatory sources checked

- [ICO guidance on collecting and keeping employment records](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/employment-practices-and-data-protection-keeping-employment-records/collecting-and-keeping-employment-records/)
  requires a documented lawful basis and purpose, data minimisation, accuracy,
  category-specific retention, transparency, and need-to-know access. Special
  category and criminal-offence data require additional Article 9 / Schedule 1
  safeguards; consent is generally invalid in employment relationships due to the power imbalance.
- [ICO guidance on workers' health information](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/information-about-workers-health/data-protection-and-workers-health-information/)
  calls for high-assurance security and separate access boundaries. Routine managers
  must receive only operational fitness and adjustment instructions needed to manage work,
  never medical diagnoses, symptoms, or occupational health evidence.
- [Working Time Regulations 1998, regulation 16B](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1833/regulation/16B)
  requires adequate records of annual leave and holiday pay to be retained for six
  years from creation. Official holiday-pay guidance requires accurate records of leave
  taken and corresponding pay based on worker patterns.
- [The Home Office right-to-work checklist](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/right-to-work-checklist/employers-right-to-work-checklist-accessible-version)
  requires prescribed checks prior to employment commencement, secure dated evidence,
  follow-up checks for time-limited immigration permissions, and retention throughout the
  engagement plus two years before secure disposal.
- [Equality Act 2010, section 20](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/section/20)
  and government workplace-adjustment guidance require reasonable adjustments where
  the statutory duty applies. Operational adjustment projections must be decoupled
  from underlying medical records and diagnostic justifications.
- [Acas Code of Practice on disciplinary and grievance procedures](https://www.acas.org.uk/acas-code-of-practice-on-disciplinary-and-grievance-procedures/html)
  sets statutory standards of procedural fairness: prompt investigations, informed
  employees, right to accompaniment, and separation of investigation, decision-maker,
  and appeal roles where practicable.
- [SRA Code of Conduct for Firms](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/code-conduct-firms)
  and [SRA continuing competence guidance](https://www.sra.org.uk/continuing-competence)
  require effective governance, competency tracking (reflection, need identification,
  planning, activity recording, and evaluation), and role-specific supervision.

## Findings

### H1 - Former-manager access persistence on manager clearance

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **High** |
| Location | `app.assert_hr_person_access` (`0059_hr_foundations.sql`) |
| Problem | The query used an inner join `JOIN app.hr_people manager ON manager.id = event.manager_person_id` prior to `ORDER BY event.effective_on DESC ... LIMIT 1`. When HR posted a clearing manager event (`manager_person_id IS NULL`), the inner join dropped the clearing row, causing PostgreSQL to fall back to the prior manager assignment event. Consequently, a former line manager whose assignment was explicitly cleared retained manager access to the direct report's leave, sickness, adjustments, and review records. |
| Impact | Unauthorised persistence of former-manager access over sensitive staff personnel records following reporting line changes. |
| Fix | **Fixed** (migration `0073`). Restructured `app.assert_hr_person_access` to query the latest `hr_manager_events` record using `LIMIT 1` prior to evaluating `manager_person_id`. If the latest event has `manager_person_id IS NULL`, manager access is immediately revoked with `42501 insufficient_privilege` (mapped to HTTP 404). |
| Test | `apps/api/src/hr/hr.controller.spec.ts` verifies that clearing a line manager (`managerPersonId: null`) immediately revokes access (`404 Not Found` on `/hr/reports/:personId` and removed from `/hr/reports`). |

### H2 - Open confidential case memberships omitted from leaver blockers

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **Medium** |
| Location | `app.hr_leaver_blockers` (`0061_hr_lifecycle_vetting.sql`) |
| Problem | The checklist completion validation blocked on active accounts, protected roles (`administrator`, `compliance`), client responsibilities, supervising partner roles, open tasks, and AML appointments, but omitted open grievance, disciplinary, and capability case memberships. |
| Impact | A departing staff member could complete their departure checklist and have their employment ended while still assigned as an active investigator, decision-maker, or appeal officer on an open confidential case. |
| Fix | **Fixed** (migration `0073`). Added the `'case_membership'` union in `app.hr_leaver_blockers`, preventing completion whenever the departing account holds an unclosed membership in an active confidential case. |
| Test | `apps/api/src/hr/hr-vetting.controller.spec.ts` verifies that active case membership is reported as a leaver blocker (`kind: 'case_membership'`). |

### H3 - Role revocation concurrency race on confidential case member checks

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **Medium** |
| Location | `app.assert_hr_case_member` (`0063_hr_cases_retention_exports.sql`) |
| Problem | When validating active HR role requirements for `hr_member` case assignments, `app.assert_hr_case_member` queried `app.account_roles` without row-level share locks. A concurrent role revocation transaction could revoke the HR role while an in-flight case transaction committed. |
| Impact | Race condition permitting case modifications during simultaneous role revocation. |
| Fix | **Fixed** (migration `0073`). Added `FOR SHARE` locks on `app.account_roles` when validating active HR role qualifications. |
| Test | Validated in migration `0073`. |

### H4 - Expired dependency age exception

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **Low** |
| Location | `dependency-age-exceptions.json` |

## Checked and in order

- **Authorization & RBAC:** Dedicated `hr` role with granular capabilities (`hr.directory`, `hr.manage`, `hr.leave`, `hr.health`, `hr.vetting`, `hr.competence`, `hr.cases`, `hr.retention`, `hr.export`). System administrators have no access to personnel records; HR users have no permissions to alter system accounts or access client/matter files.
- **Confidentiality & Encryption:** Private files and sensitive structured fields use `HR_ENCRYPTION_KEY` with distinct authenticated encryption contexts bound to record ID and purpose. Dedicated `hr/` private storage namespace.
- **Health Data Isolation:** Health records, symptoms, diagnoses, and occupational health notes are strictly isolated from general absence administration. Sickness records track dates and notifications without medical text. Operational adjustment instructions visible to line managers are strictly actionable and exclude medical justifications.
- **Bounded Line Manager Projections:** Line managers access only active direct reports through effective-dated events. Former managers are immediately cut off upon reassignment or termination. Line managers cannot view compensation, right-to-work documents, medical diagnoses, grievance cases, or full personnel histories.
- **Exact Time & Money Arithmetic:** Working patterns and annual leave balances are tracked in integer minutes with UK local calendar handling. Payroll-supplied holiday pay records are stored in integer pence (`bigint`), avoiding floating-point rounding errors.
- **Integrity & Immutability:** Personnel history, employment changes, right-to-work checks, competence reflections, appraisal acknowledgements, and case proceedings are append-only. Triggers prevent updates and deletions.
- **Four-Eyes Principle on Exports:** HR export bundles require preparation and approval by distinct HR members before download. Preparers cannot approve their own exports.
- **Retention & Controlled Disposal:** Category retention rules enforce statutory baselines (6 years for leave and pay records, engagement duration plus 2 years for right-to-work evidence). Active legal holds block disposal. Controlled disposal requires typed `DISPOSE` confirmation and records content-free tombstones.

# Security review: Artificial Intelligence and Legal Drafting Assistant module

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Scope:** AI steps 18a to 18f, migration `0074_ai_foundations.sql`, `apps/api/src/ai/`, staff AI workbench in `apps/web/src/app/(staff)/matters/[matterId]/ai-workbench-section.tsx`, supervisory governance dashboard in `apps/web/src/app/(staff)/compliance/ai/`, provider-neutral `OfflineMockAiProvider` boundary, and adversarial penetration test suite in `apps/api/src/ai/ai-security-review.spec.ts`.  
**Method:** SRA Standards & Regulations, SRA guidance on generative AI in practice, Legal Professional Privilege (LPP) principles, and UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018 (Articles 5, 22) were evaluated against the system architecture. Threat vectors spanning indirect prompt injection, XML delimiter breakouts, cross-matter ethical wall leakage, sensitive client data exposure, denial-of-wallet quota exhaustion, hallucinated citation risks, and unauthorized autonomous legal actions were audited and verified with automated test suites.

## Regulatory sources checked

- [SRA Code of Conduct for Solicitors, RELs and RFLs (paragraphs 1.4, 2.1, 3.2, 6.1, 8.6)](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/standards-regulations/code-conduct-solicitors/)
  - Para 1.4: Duty not to mislead clients or the court; fee earners remain personally accountable for accuracy of all submissions and correspondence.
  - Para 2.1: Duty of competence and standard of service.
  - Para 3.2: Cooperation with supervisors; effective supervision of delegated or automated tasks.
  - Para 6.1: Duty of confidentiality and protection of Legal Professional Privilege.
  - Para 8.6: Clear disclosure of costs, services, and handling methods.
- [SRA Guidance: The use of artificial intelligence in the legal sector](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/use-artificial-intelligence-legal-sector/)
  - Mandates human oversight on all client-facing or court-facing drafts.
  - Prohibits transmitting client or matter data to public consumer AI models or services that retain prompts for training.
  - Requires independent verification of AI-generated case law, citations, and legal authorities against primary sources ([legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/), [BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/)).
- [UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/)
  - Article 5(1)(c) (Data Minimisation): Ingesting only strictly necessary facts for the drafting task.
  - Article 22: Right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing having legal or significant effects.

## Findings & Architectural Hardening

### AI1 - Indirect Prompt Injection & Delimiter Escapes

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **High** |
| Threat | Counterparty documents or emails containing prompt-injection payloads (e.g. `</untrusted_matter_document>`, "IGNORE ALL PREVIOUS INSTRUCTIONS", system prompt disclosure requests). |
| Hardening | `PromptBuilderService.escapeDelimiters` sanitizes all closing tags to safe XML entities (`&lt;/untrusted_matter_document&gt;`). System prompt explicitly directs the LLM to treat matter content strictly as passive evidence. Heuristic injection pattern detector intercepts attack signatures and records `ai.prompt_injection_detected` compliance audit alerts. Verified in `ai-security-review.spec.ts`. |

### AI2 - Cross-Matter Ethical Wall & Domain Isolation

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **High** |
| Threat | Attacker providing context document IDs from Matter B while drafting in Matter A, or attempting to ingest HR personnel records or AML suspicion reports into AI prompts. |
| Hardening | `AiService.generateDraft` enforces `AiRepository.validateMatterDocumentOwnership`. Referenced documents not belonging to the active matter immediately trigger `ForbiddenException` before contacting the provider. Database gate `app.assert_matter_ai_access` enforces ethical walls against unassigned staff. Tables in `hr.*` and `compliance.restricted_reports` have zero AI endpoints or foreign keys. |

### AI3 - Pre-Prompt Sensitive Data Minimisation

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **High** |
| Threat | Transmitting sensitive personal identifiers, bank sort codes, payment cards, or credentials to AI models in violation of UK GDPR Article 5(1)(c). |
| Hardening | `RedactionService` scrubs UK National Insurance numbers (including HMRC test prefix QQ), Visa/Mastercard/Amex card numbers, bank sort codes, bank account numbers, passwords, API keys, and explicit DOBs into standardized tokens (`[REDACTED_*]`) prior to prompt assembly. Statute and case citations are preserved without false-positive redaction. |

### AI4 - Denial-of-Wallet & Token Budget Exhaustion

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **Medium** |
| Threat | Oversized requests or runaway automated generation creating excessive costs or exhausting monthly API quota. |
| Hardening | Pre-flight token estimation rejects requests exceeding per-request limits (4,096 tokens) with `BadRequestException` prior to external calls. Monthly firm budget (100,000 tokens) and per-matter token quota (150,000 tokens) enforce hard stops via `ForbiddenException`. Emergency kill-switch in `app.ai_configurations` halts all generations firm-wide immediately. |

### AI5 - Non-Autonomous Human-in-the-Loop Authority

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **High** |
| Threat | AI autonomously dispensing legal advice, communicating with clients, or directly creating unvetted court documents. |
| Hardening | AI outputs are strictly created with status `unapproved_draft`. A human review state machine (`draft` -> `accepted` | `modified` | `rejected`) requires explicit sign-off by a qualified fee earner. Promotion to official matter documents (`app.documents`) and matter tasks (`app.tasks`) is strictly blocked for rejected drafts. |

### AI6 - Hallucination Defense & Grounded Citation Verification

| | |
|---|---|
| Severity | **Medium** |
| Threat | AI hallucinating fictitious case citations, statutes, or contractual clauses (violating SRA Code paras 1.4 & 2.1). |
| Hardening | `CitationGroundingService` compares normalized quoted excerpts against matter context documents using exact substring matching, then a fallback requiring at least 85% of quote words to occur as substrings in the source. Unverified citations attach statutory warning notices to the draft, trigger `ai.citation_unverified` audit warnings, and highlight ungrounded text in the web workbench. |
